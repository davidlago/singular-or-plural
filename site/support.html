Support

Found a problem, a sentence that's wrong, or have an idea? Open an issue at github.com/davidlago/singular-or-plural/issues (a free GitHub account is needed). Please include your iPhone model and iOS version.

How it works

Which way do I swipe?

Left is always the singular form (one), right is always the plural form (many). The two buttons under the card work too.

How is the score counted?

A right answer is worth 50 points plus up to 50 more for speed. The bonus drains while you think, and it takes longer sentences into account. Streaks multiply your points: ×1.5 from 5 in a row, ×2 from 10, ×3 from 20. A wrong answer costs 25 points and resets the streak.

How do I get the third star?

Get at least 95% right and answer at or under par (the expected time for each sentence's length). The round summary always says what the next star needs.

How do I open the next level?

Each level has four rounds. A round counts when it gets two stars (90% right). When all four count, the Boss round opens: 15 cards on a clock, with at most two misses. Beat it to open the next level.

What's the Fix-it pile?

Every missed sentence goes there and comes back the next day, then in 3 days, then in a week, until it's been answered right and fast.

Parent corner

How do I get in?

Tap the gear on the home screen. The first time, you choose a 4-digit PIN.

I forgot the PIN

The PIN can't be recovered. Deleting and reinstalling the app resets it, and also deletes all progress.

Can I add my own sentences?

Yes: in the Parent corner, type the sentence with ___ for the blank, the singular and plural forms, and which one is right. It joins the rounds of the level you choose.
