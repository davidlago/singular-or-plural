Privacy Policy

For the One or Many iPhone app. Last updated November 7, 2026.

The short version

One or Many collects nothing. There's no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no server. The app never connects to the internet.

What the app stores, and where

To show scores, stars, stats and the Fix-it pile, the app saves your answers on your iPhone: which sentence, which side you chose, whether it was right and how long it took. It also saves its settings, the Parent corner PIN and any sentences a parent adds. All of this stays in the app's own storage on the device. It's never sent anywhere by the app.

Like every app's data, it can be included in your own iPhone backups (iCloud or your computer), under Apple's privacy policy. The developer can't see it.

Deleting your data

In the app: Parent corner ▸ Reset all progress deletes scores, stars, stats and the Fix-it pile. Deleting the app from your iPhone deletes everything it stored.

Children

One or Many is made for learners of all ages, including children. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, there's nothing to share, sell or disclose. The app has no ads, no in-app purchases, no links to websites and no ways to contact other people.

This website

This site is hosted by GitHub Pages and loads nothing from other servers. GitHub may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) under GitHub's privacy statement.

Changes and questions

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions: open an issue at github.com/davidlago/singular-or-plural/issues.
